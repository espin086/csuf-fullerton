# Replication and evaluation

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every model has simulation, parity and published-result cases

The replication registry SHALL hold, for each of M0, M1, M2 and M3, at least one simulation
recovery case, one software parity case and one published-result or regression case, each with
a named source, fixture hash, metrics, tolerances and seed.

#### Scenario: Registry completeness
- **WHEN** `csuf-fullerton replicate --list` runs
- **THEN** it SHALL list every case by id, model, kind and suite, and SHALL exit non-zero if any
  model lacks one of the three kinds

### Requirement: Simulation recovery meets declared targets

Simulation cases SHALL estimate the model on data drawn from a known DGP and compare bias, RMSE,
band coverage and test size with the targets in the case file.

#### Scenario: M1 band coverage
- **WHEN** the full suite runs the M1 recovery case with 200 replications at T = 400
- **THEN** 90% bootstrap bands SHALL cover the true frozen-year IRFs, and their difference,
  in at least 85% of replications

#### Scenario: LR test size
- **WHEN** data are simulated with `G = 0`
- **THEN** the constant-vs-interacted LR test SHALL reject at the 5% level in between 3% and 8%
  of replications

### Requirement: Results match reference implementations

Parity cases SHALL compare against statsmodels VAR, the statsmodels Kalman smoother, and this
package's own 0002 estimator, using fixtures generated by checked-in scripts. A fixture from R
`bvarsv` MAY be added later; until then the Primiceri (2005) published case covers the full
stochastic-volatility sampler.

#### Scenario: statsmodels VAR parity
- **WHEN** M0 runs unrestricted on the parity fixture
- **THEN** coefficients SHALL match `statsmodels.tsa.api.VAR` within 1e-8

#### Scenario: Kalman smoother parity
- **WHEN** the M3 coefficient step runs with fixed covariances on simulated data
- **THEN** its posterior mean SHALL match the statsmodels Kalman smoother within 1e-8

### Requirement: Published results are reproduced in direction and size

Published-result cases SHALL encode each paper's headline claim as numeric thresholds in the
case file and mark whether the check is exact or qualitative.

#### Scenario: Primiceri volatility peak
- **WHEN** M3 runs on U.S. inflation, unemployment and the interest rate, 1953Q1 to 2001Q3
- **THEN** the posterior median interest-rate volatility SHALL peak between 1979Q1 and 1983Q4

#### Scenario: China effect rises with trade weight
- **WHEN** M1 runs on the Brazil fixture through 2009-12 with 1995 and 2009 frozen weights
- **THEN** the long-run Brazil GDP response to a China GDP shock SHALL be larger with 2009
  weights over months 0 to 24, and the response to a U.S. activity shock SHALL be smaller

### Requirement: The pipeline fails loudly and leaves a scorecard

`replicate` SHALL write `scorecard.json` and `replication.md`, exit non-zero when any metric
misses its tolerance or any fixture hash differs, and compare against pinned golden values once
a case is pinned.

#### Scenario: Fixture tampering
- **WHEN** a fixture file's SHA-256 does not match its case file
- **THEN** the case SHALL be reported as failed, not skipped, and the command SHALL exit non-zero

#### Scenario: Unintended numerical drift
- **WHEN** a code change moves a pinned value beyond its golden tolerance but inside the economic
  threshold
- **THEN** the case SHALL fail and the report SHALL show expected and observed values

#### Scenario: Fast suite in the PR gate
- **WHEN** `make check` runs
- **THEN** the fast suite SHALL run, finish in under 3 minutes on a CI runner, and fail the check
  on any failed case
